Proshno

Internet chara eta kivabe chole?

Net thakle message internet diye jay. Na thakle WiFi ba LAN, tomar ISP er nijer line, ar desher bhitorer network: je ta khola thake sheta diye. Shob bondho hole shudhu same WiFi te cholbe.

Phone e ki app lagbe?

Na. Kacher computer er QR scan korle browser ei khule jay. Android app pore ashbe.

Amar message ki keu porte pare?

Na, message end-to-end encrypted. Internet diye gele majher relay gula shudhu encrypted lekha dekhe. Tobe ei version e forward secrecy nai: tomar key churi hole purono message pora jete pare. Ar phone diye join korle message oi computer e thake, je chalay she dekhte pare.

Taka lagbe?

Na. Free, open source, MIT license.

Windows warning dekhacche keno?

Notun app e SmartScreen warning aste pare. Chalanor age publisher ar checksum miliye nao. Sign kora thakleo warning aste pare; Windows protection bondho koro na.

Net thakle-o ki eta kaje lage?

Ha. Eta protidin er app: net thakle internet diye chole, ar net gele kichu bodlate hoy na, nijei Beacon network e chole jay.
